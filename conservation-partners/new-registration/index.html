Conservation Partner Playbook: New Registration

Your guide to running new registrations: where to go, what to do, how to make changes, and how to get help. Last verified 2 Nov 2026.
This is your system for managing new Conservation Partners.Registrations, payments, partner details and follow-up tasks now come together in one organized process. Instead of tracking information across separate forms, emails and spreadsheets, you can open your Airtable workspace, see what needs attention, and work through the next steps. The aim is simple: less tracking, more time for partners.

Your regular workflow

  1. Open Start Here and look at the numbers.
  2. Review anything that needs attention: fee reduction requests, held items, toolkits to ship, listings to review.
  3. Process registrations and follow-up: decide fee reductions, ship toolkits, publish directory listings, send coupons.
  4. Update partner information and fulfillment status as you go, so the next person sees the current picture.
  5. Once a week, ask store or operations staff, who have PayPal access, to confirm the PayPal payments match the 2027 Partners list. Nothing alerts anyone if the automation pauses, so this quick check keeps you covered.
  6. Use the "I need to..." tab when you want steps for something less familiar.

What it does for you

  • Less typing. Each registration is saved in Airtable automatically and matched to the organization and contact if they already exist (tested 29 Oct).
  • One place for partner information. Partners and their years of participation are in the same workspace.
  • A clear view of new registrations. The 2027 Partners list shows status, payment, toolkit and shipping at a glance.
  • Visible to-dos. Start Here counts the work waiting, so nothing depends on memory or an inbox search.
  • Fewer missed registrations. partners@reef.org gets an alert for each one (tested 29 Oct), and anything unusual is held with a plain-English note.
  • Consistent communication. Every new partner gets the same welcome email automatically.

Coming later: partner contact records and follow-up emails in Zoho CRM and Zoho Campaigns. These are planned, not live yet.

What you can do on your own

Most of the work is yours to run: reviewing registrations, deciding fee reductions, shipping toolkits, publishing directory listings, correcting partner information, sending coupons, and updating wording on the form, the welcome email and your REEF.org pages. You can also improve your own procedures as you learn what works. A few changes touch payments or the connections between systems. Those are marked Coordinate before changing, with the reason and who can help.

Working in the shared Airtable workspace

You use the shared REEF Staff login. The Conservation Partners pages are built for your daily work, and you can edit records on them. The Data and Automations tabs hold the connected workflow that moves registrations along; a change there can affect every registration, so stay in the interface pages. Because the login is shared, change records rather than shared views. If you want a view of your own, create a new one and leave the existing ones as they are.

A few safeguards, and why they exist

  • Payments and refunds. A refund moves money out of PayPal, so store or operations staff process it. You talk to the partner and make the request.
  • Deleting records. A deletion cannot be undone and breaks the link to payments and history. Correct or merge instead. If a record really needs to be removed, technical support can do it safely.
  • Test submissions. The live form takes real PayPal payments and writes to live records. Use Preview in Zoho Forms to check wording. If you need a full end-to-end test, ask technical support to set one up with TEST- records.
  • Adding a registration by hand. Payments are matched to partners through the form. If a partner cannot use the form, technical support can add them properly.

When you need help

Questions about partners, shipping, listings and wording are yours to answer. Payment and PayPal questions go to store or operations staff. Problems with the form, the connections or access go to technical support (currently Martha). Emails sent from Zoho Campaigns go through Tom. The full list is on the Reference tab. When you ask for help, include the organization, a screenshot and what you were trying to do.

What happens when someone registers

  1. The partner fills in the form and pays. After paying, they should land on a thank-you page.
  2. Zoho Flow puts one row in the Airtable Registrations Inbox. The Inbox keeps the original submission and feeds the automation, which is why corrections go on the partner record instead.
  3. Airtable marks it paid, finds or creates the organization and the contact, and saves a 2027 registration. Fee reduction requests wait for your decision.
  4. Anything unusual is held with a plain note and shows on Start Here.
  5. The partner gets a welcome email, partners@ gets an alert, and PayPal sends its own receipt.
Held does not mean broken"Name is close to an existing partner" and "Already registered for this year" often show up on a registration that worked. Airtable holds these for a person to confirm instead of guessing. Read the note, check, then follow the Next Step.

How to make changes

Wording, content and your own procedures are yours to change.Changes that touch payments or the connections between systems get a quick coordination step, so registrations keep flowing. Each card says what you can change, where to go, how, what to check afterward, and when to bring in help. Help us improve the workflow

This system is a foundation, not a finished product. If something feels cumbersome, confusing or unnecessarily manual, flag it. The goal is to keep improving the workflow so it supports the way the program actually works. Decide which kind of change it is:

  • Something you can change yourself: wording, content, your own procedures. Just do it.
  • An interface improvement you would like: a new view, a clearer label, a missing column.
  • A new automation that could save time.
  • A problem that needs troubleshooting: use the Fixes tab first.
  • A policy decision that needs a wider conversation: fees, refunds, invoicing.

For the middle three, add a short task to the Conservation Partners project in Asana: what you were trying to do and what would help.
