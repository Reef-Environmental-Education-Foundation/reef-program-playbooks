Registrations and payments arrive in one place, matched to the right people and teams, so you spend less time chasing information across forms, PayPal and spreadsheets. You own the program and its day-to-day work. The technology is there to support you.

Protect the connections

A few things are protected because other systems depend on them. Each has a reason, and each has a way to get the job done.

  • Do not delete an Inbox row or set a Processed row back to New. The Inbox row is the permanent record of what the captain submitted and paid. Running it again would create a second registration. To fix people or teams, edit the Team or person record instead.
  • Run test submissions only with the TEST- procedure. A test payment is real money and writes to the live Inbox. Practice on the sample records and the TEST CLONE instead (see the practice session on the Reference tab).
  • Payment settings and payment fixes go through the right role. A duplicate, mistaken or wrong-price payment is handled with Store and Finance, not by editing PayPal or the payment fields. See Payments, refunds and exceptions on the Reference tab.

What happens when a team registers

  1. The captain fills in the Zoho form and pays in PayPal. The Flow runs only after a successful payment.
  2. Zoho Flow puts one row in the Airtable Registrations Inbox.
  3. Airtable marks it paid when the PayPal transaction ID arrives, finds or creates each person and the team, and creates the registration and roster.
  4. Anything unusual is held in Needs Attention with a plain note. The registration may still exist.
  5. The captain gets a confirmation email and a PayPal receipt.
Normal flags, not problems"PRICE CHECK (registration WAS created)", "possible duplicate person" and "near-match name" can appear on a registration that worked. Read the note, confirm, then mark it reviewed.

Common questions and fixes

Most of these you can handle yourself. Each card shows what happened, what to check first, what you can do, how to confirm it is fixed, and who can help when it is not.

Before you ask technical coordination for help with a Flow, an Airtable automation, PayPal or the form connection, collect:
  • The team or organization name and the registrant's email address.
  • The date and time (with time zone) of the payment or submission.
  • The PayPal transaction ID, if there was a payment.
  • The Inbox Submission ID or Zoho entry, if you can see one.
  • A screenshot of what you see, and a line on what you expected.
  • Whether more than one team or vendor is affected.

Payments, refunds and exceptions

REEF does not issue refunds. That is the approved policy for team cancellations, and it is preserved here. Other payment situations are different from a cancellation and are handled one at a time, with the right role. You decide exceptions and payment cases. Store and Finance process any refund, and you do not change payment settings or PayPal yourself.

When something comes up, name what kind of thing it is. That tells us who picks it up.

KindExampleWhat happens
Routine changeNew wording in an email; a team name typoYou make it yourself. Use the card.
Interface improvementA page that is missing, a field you cannot edit, a filter that hides what you needAdd it to the improvement list.
Potential automationA reminder you keep sending by handAdd it to the improvement list. Nothing automated starts without explicit approval.
Technical problemA payment with no record; a form that will not loadUse the Fixes tab, collect the details, and contact technical coordination.

    Known limitations

    • No real 2027 registration has gone through the live system yet.
    • Rosters have no page for viewing or editing yet. Captain contact corrections have no Derby page yet.
    • The staff alert to lionfish@reef.org is built but untested. The Zoho Flow failure notice goes to the flow owner only.
    • Waiver tracking, vendor acceptance and payment, and all vendor emails are not built.
    • Abandoned-checkout capture is not connected, so teams who start paying and quit are not recorded.
    • Airtable pages cannot lock individual fields. Edit only what the page shows, and leave payment fields alone unless a card tells you to change them.
